Free & legal streaming hub

Why pay for five subscriptions when great content is free?

Plenty of movies, shows and anime are free and legal to watch. Here are trusted places to find them, and how to avoid the fake "free" sites that put your device at risk.

The problem

Streaming got expensive and scattered

Subscription fatigue

Each service wants its own monthly fee. Add a few together and the bill rivals the old cable package.

Content split across apps

The show you want is always on the one service you don't have, so people keep signing up and cancelling.

"Free" sites that cost you

Unofficial streaming sites push fake download buttons, pop-ups and redirects that lead to malware and stolen passwords.

The fix: use official free services. They are supported by ads or by your library, they are legal, and they don't put your device at risk.
